Real-world problems

Problems businesses face. How we fix them.

These are the issues we see most often on Shopify stores and React sites, and what we do about each one.

✕ Problem

"My store takes forever to load."

Heavy themes, oversized images and a dozen apps add seconds to every page, and many mobile visitors leave before it finishes.

✓ Our fix

Speed-first rebuild

We compress images to modern formats, lazy-load below the fold, remove unused apps and inline critical styles.

Goal: under 1 second
✕ Problem

"People add to cart but never buy."

Hidden shipping costs, forced sign-ups and clunky cart pages make buyers give up at the last step.

✓ Our fix

Friction-free checkout

Slide-out cart, shipping shown up front, guest checkout, UPI and wallet payments, and a free-shipping progress bar.

Fewer abandoned carts
✕ Problem

"It looks broken on phones."

Most shoppers browse on mobile, but many sites are designed on desktop and squeezed down afterwards.

✓ Our fix

Mobile-first design

We design for the phone first: thumb-friendly buttons, readable text and layouts tested on real devices.

Works on every screen
✕ Problem

"Nobody finds us on Google."

Slow pages, missing titles and content that only renders in the browser keep sites off the first page of results.

✓ Our fix

SEO built in

Server-rendered pages, clean URLs, structured data for products and reviews, sitemaps and Core Web Vitals tuning.

Search-ready from day one
✕ Problem

"I need a developer for every small change."

Changing a banner, price or photo shouldn't mean waiting days for a developer and paying for it.

✓ Our fix

Edit it yourself

Drag-and-drop Shopify sections or a simple CMS for React sites, plus a short video walkthrough so your team is confident.

Updates in minutes
✕ Problem

"Our React app has become a mess."

Every page has slightly different buttons and forms, so new features take longer and bugs keep coming back.

✓ Our fix

One design system

A shared, documented component library with consistent colours, spacing and behaviour across your whole product.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Should I choose Shopify or a React / Next.js website?

If your main goal is selling products online, Shopify is usually the best choice because payments, inventory and shipping are built in. If you're a service business, startup or SaaS, a Next.js site gives you more flexibility. For very large stores we can combine both in a "headless" setup.

Can you speed up my existing Shopify store?

Yes. We start with a free audit that shows what's slowing your store down, such as heavy apps, images or theme code, then fix it without changing how you manage your store.

Will I be able to edit the website myself?

Absolutely. Shopify stores use drag-and-drop sections, and React/Next.js sites can connect to a simple content editor. Every project includes a training walkthrough.

How long does a project take?

A landing page typically takes 1–2 weeks, a full Shopify store 3–5 weeks, and custom web apps depend on scope. You'll get a clear timeline before we start.

Do you provide hosting and support after launch?

Shopify includes hosting. For React/Next.js sites we deploy to fast global hosting such as Vercel or Netlify. All projects include 30 days of free support, with optional monthly plans after that.

Ready for a website that works as hard as you do?

Tell us a little about your project. We'll reply within one business day with ideas and a free audit of your current site.
