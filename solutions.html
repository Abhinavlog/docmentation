✓ One video at a time, posters for the rest

02

Jumping layouts

You go to tap a button and, at the last moment, an image or banner loads above it and pushes everything down. You tap the wrong thing. Google measures this as Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) and ranks jumpy pages lower.

✕ Old wayContent jumps

✕ Content loads late and shoves things around

The page shows text first, then an ad and an image pop in with no space reserved. The Buy button moves just as the user taps, and they hit the ad.

✓ Our wayNothing moves
Buy now✓ Added
Tapped Buy. Nothing moved.

✓ Space is reserved before anything loads

The image area is held open from the start with a shimmering placeholder. When the photo arrives it fades in, and the button stays exactly where it was.

Fixed aspect ratiosEvery image and video has its width and height set, so its space is known up front.
Skeleton placeholdersGrey shapes hold the layout and show that content is on its way.
No surprise insertsBanners and ads get reserved slots, or appear below what the user is reading.
CLS monitoringWe check layout shift in Lighthouse and real-user data before launch.

Used in: Aurelia Skin, Brewline Coffee, Pulse Analytics
