Pulse Analytics
The challenge

What was holding them back

After years of quick features, every screen had different buttons, tables and forms. The dashboard showed a blank screen while data loaded, then everything jumped into place.

Goal: Create a single component library the team can build with, and make the dashboard feel instant even with heavy data.

What we built
1

Design system

A shared library of buttons, inputs, tables, modals and toasts with light and dark themes.

2

Skeleton dashboards

Charts and KPI cards show placeholders the instant the page opens, then fill in without moving.

3

Accessible data tables

Sortable, keyboard-friendly tables with sticky headers and clear empty states.

4

Toasts and inline feedback

Every save, export and error gets clear, friendly confirmation.
