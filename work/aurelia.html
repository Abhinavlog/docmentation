Aurelia Skin
The challenge

What was holding them back

The store ran on a heavy theme with more than a dozen apps. Product pages were slow on phones, ingredient info was a wall of text, and adding to the cart reloaded the whole page.

Goal: Make the store feel premium and instant on mobile, and make choosing a product for your skin type effortless.

What we built
1

Variant picker by skin type

Shoppers pick Dry, Oily or Combination and the photo, price and stock update instantly.

2

Cart drawer with free-shipping bar

Adding to cart opens a side drawer. A progress bar shows how close the order is to free delivery.

3

Ingredient accordion

Long ingredient and how-to-use info folded into tidy sections that open on tap.

4

App consolidation

Reviews, upsells and badges rebuilt as native theme sections to remove app bloat.
